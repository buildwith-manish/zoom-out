VOICEOVER SCRIPT - Zoom Out: Food Edition (full video, start to finish)
=======================================================================
Voice: upbeat, warm, game-show host. Natural, conversational, everyday phrasing.
Save each clip under the filename shown, in public/voiceover/
Formats: .wav .mp3 .m4a .aac .ogg .flac      Then run: npm run voiceover

Total clips: 112  =  intro 1 + qq 50 + q 50 + bonusq 5 + bonus 5 + outro 1 + sting 1


PLAYBACK ORDER (so you can record in sequence)
----------------------------------------------
intro > [Easy card] Q1-Q10 > Bonus 1 > Q11-Q20 > Bonus 2 > [Medium card] Q21-Q30
> Bonus 3 > Q31-Q35 > [Hard card] Q36-Q40 > Bonus 4 > Q41-Q50 > Bonus 5
> outro > sting

Level cards (Easy / Medium / Hard) have no clip slot. Their feel is carried by
the first question line of each level (qq01, qq21, qq36).

  zoom. Land "Ready?" just as the full picture reveals.

RECORDING NOTES

Tone
  - qq##   every line is different on purpose. Keep it curious and a little
           teasing, never flat. Leave the silence after the line alone, the
           zoom does the talking.
  - q##    answer first, punchy like a buzzer, then a 2-3 word tag on the
           end. Pause one beat between the answer and the tag.
  - bonusq a notch more excited. This is the special round. The comment
           request is the whole point, so say it clearly.
  - bonus# answer plus one short reaction, then stop.
  - intro  hook in the first two seconds.
  - outro  talk to one person, not a crowd.

Question lines name the same category the screen shows (food, fruit, dessert,
snack, breakfast, vegetable, treat, pastry, nut, seafood), but they are not all
questions, and no line gives the answer away.

Don't say anything during the 8-second bonus clock. The tick sounds carry it.
No clips for the hint (it appears at 40% of the zoom). It stays visual.

Heads up: `npm run voiceover:script` regenerates this file from
questions.json and will overwrite it.
