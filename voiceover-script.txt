Naming convention:
  qq01.wav ... qq50.wav  = question-phase clips (plays before the zoom/timer)
  q01.wav  ... q50.wav   = reveal clips (plays at the reveal moment)
  intro.wav, outro.wav, sting.wav = intro/outro/sting clips
  (.mp3 .wav .m4a .aac .ogg .flac all work)

After recording, run:
  npm run voiceover    # scan clips + build src/voiceover-manifest.json
  The video timeline auto-stretches to fit each clip.
